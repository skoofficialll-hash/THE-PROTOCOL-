I

GENERAL CONDUCT & COMMUNICATION PROTOCOLS

18 clauses

1.1The Silence Over Drama Standard

ZERO-MERCY members are expected to maintain composure and professionalism in public channels and external communities.

  • Unnecessary arguments, spam, meaningless messages and low-level trolling are prohibited.
  • Do not engage with people attempting to provoke, bait or create unnecessary drama.
  • Maintain dignity during disagreements. Silence and disengagement are often better than escalating a conflict.
  • Members must not use ZERO-MERCY's name to start or continue public disputes.

1.2Zero Tolerance for Low-Quality Communication

  • Excessive or unnecessary pinging is prohibited.
  • Toxic language, targeted insults, harassment and discriminatory remarks are not acceptable.
  • Do not flood channels with repeated messages, reactions, images or advertisements.
  • Use channels according to their designated purpose.
  • Respect other members' time and communication boundaries.

1.3Respect & Professional Behaviour

  • Treat every member with basic respect, regardless of rank, experience, skill or popularity.
  • Bullying, intimidation, humiliation and targeted harassment are prohibited.
  • Do not misuse another person's personal information.
  • Disagreements must remain respectful and must not become personal attacks.

1.4Organisational Hierarchy & Chain of Command

  • Follow legitimate instructions issued by authorised leadership and staff.
  • Do not impersonate founders, administrators or other officials.
  • Organisational decisions should be discussed through the appropriate internal channels.
  • Members may respectfully question decisions and submit grievances through official procedures.
  • No staff member may use their rank to threaten, intimidate or silence legitimate complaints.
II

GAMEPLAY, SCRIMS & PERFORMANCE STANDARDS

19 clauses

2.1Performance Over Presence

  • A title, rank, gang tag or reputation does not automatically establish skill.
  • Members should demonstrate ability through gameplay, teamwork, execution and discipline.
  • False claims about achievements, rankings or competitive experience are prohibited.
  • Respect the efforts and achievements of other players.

2.2Mandatory Event & Custom Participation

  • Registered active-roster members are expected to attend officially scheduled mandatory events.
  • Members must communicate in advance if they cannot attend.
  • Genuine emergencies, technical problems and approved absences will be considered.
  • Repeated unexplained absences may affect roster eligibility.
  • Event participation requirements must be communicated before an event begins.

2.3Match Conduct

  • Harassment, personal insults and discriminatory remarks toward opponents are prohibited.
  • Do not provoke opponents through abusive messages or repeated unwanted contact.
  • Follow the rules and format of each custom match, scrim or tournament.
  • Do not exploit bugs or use unauthorised software to gain an unfair advantage.
  • Respect match hosts, referees and verified results.

2.4Fair Play & Competitive Integrity

The following are prohibited:

  • Cheats, hacks, scripts and unauthorised modifications.
  • Match fixing or deliberate manipulation of results.
  • Fake screenshots or fabricated performance records.
  • Unauthorised account sharing where prohibited by the game's rules.
  • Deliberate disruption of official matches.
III

VETTING & RECRUITMENT PROTOCOL

17 clauses

3.1Controlled Recruitment

  • Entry into official competitive rosters and organisational positions may require an application, invitation or assessment.
  • Recruitment decisions are based on conduct, reliability, skill and suitability for the relevant role.
  • No applicant is guaranteed acceptance.
  • Applicants must provide accurate information.

3.2Evaluation & Observation

Applicants may be evaluated on:

  • Communication and teamwork.
  • Behaviour under pressure.
  • Gameplay performance, where relevant.
  • Understanding of organisational rules.
  • Reliability and commitment.
  • Respect for other players and staff.

3.3Recruitment Integrity

  • Fake identities, false achievements and misleading applications are prohibited.
  • Bribery, threats or pressure to obtain a position are prohibited.
  • No applicant may claim official membership before receiving confirmation.
  • Recruitment assessments must be conducted fairly and consistently.

3.4Confidentiality During Recruitment

  • Do not share private interview questions, internal evaluation material or confidential recruitment discussions without authorisation.
  • Do not publish another applicant's private information.
  • Recruitment decisions must be communicated through authorised channels.
IV

SAFETY, HARASSMENT & ZERO-TOLERANCE CONTENT

22 clauses

4.1Child Safety & Sexual Exploitation

ZERO-MERCY strictly prohibits all forms of child sexual exploitation and abuse.

The following are strictly forbidden:

Any suspected child sexual exploitation incident must be reported immediately to Discord and the appropriate authorities. Do not download, forward or redistribute suspected illegal material.

  • Any sexual content involving minors.
  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM), including requesting, sharing, storing or distributing it.
  • Grooming, sexual solicitation or attempts to exploit minors.
  • Sexual threats, coercion or blackmail involving minors.
  • Attempts to arrange sexual contact with minors.
  • Sharing links or instructions intended to facilitate child exploitation.

4.2Sexual Harassment & Explicit Content

  • Unwanted sexual messages, comments or advances are prohibited.
  • Sharing explicit images or videos without consent is prohibited.
  • Sending unsolicited sexual content is prohibited.
  • Sexual harassment, coercion and threats are prohibited.
  • Explicit adult content is not permitted in ZERO-MERCY spaces.

4.3Hate, Threats & Discrimination

  • Racism, religious hatred, caste-based abuse and other discriminatory conduct are prohibited.
  • Threats of violence, targeted intimidation and stalking are prohibited.
  • Encouraging harm against another person or group is prohibited.
  • Doxxing and threats to expose private information are strictly prohibited.

4.4Self-Harm & Dangerous Content

  • Do not encourage, glorify or pressure anyone into self-harm or dangerous challenges.
  • Do not share instructions intended to harm people.
  • Members should report content that creates an immediate safety concern.

4.5Reporting Safety Incidents

  • Report serious incidents through the official support or moderation system.
  • Do not publicly expose victims, reporters or sensitive evidence.
  • Staff must handle reports discreetly and escalate serious safety incidents appropriately.
  • Retaliation against anyone who makes a good-faith report is prohibited.
V

PRIVACY, SECURITY & CONFIDENTIALITY

18 clauses

5.1Personal Information Protection

  • Never publish another member's real name, address, phone number, email, account credentials or private conversations without permission.
  • Do not request passwords, authentication codes or recovery codes.
  • Do not share private screenshots or recordings without appropriate consent.
  • Members should avoid sharing sensitive personal information in public channels.

5.2Internal Confidentiality

The following information must remain within authorised organisational channels:

Unauthorised disclosure may result in disciplinary action, depending on the seriousness of the incident.

  • Internal strategies and operational plans.
  • Private event arrangements and custom setups.
  • Unpublished organisational decisions.
  • Staff discussions and confidential reports.
  • Private recruitment and disciplinary records.
  • Security procedures and access credentials.

5.3Account & Server Security

  • Do not share Discord account credentials or bot tokens.
  • Do not click suspicious links or download untrusted files.
  • Do not impersonate official ZERO-MERCY accounts.
  • Do not attempt to compromise accounts, bots or server infrastructure.
  • Report suspicious activity to authorised staff.

5.4Recordings & Screenshots

  • Do not record or publish private voice conversations without appropriate consent.
  • Do not manipulate screenshots or recordings to falsely accuse another member.
  • Evidence submitted in reports must not be altered or deliberately misrepresented.
VI

STAFF, MODERATOR & ADMINISTRATION RULES

22 clauses

6.1Staff Responsibilities

All staff members must:

  • Act professionally and impartially.
  • Follow the official rules and moderation procedures.
  • Respond to assigned responsibilities within a reasonable time.
  • Protect confidential information.
  • Avoid unnecessary conflict with members.
  • Escalate serious incidents to the appropriate authority.

6.2Abuse of Authority

The following are prohibited:

  • Using permissions for personal revenge.
  • Threatening members with administrative action.
  • Issuing punishments without a legitimate reason.
  • Deleting or hiding evidence to protect a friend.
  • Misusing private information.
  • Claiming authority beyond the assigned role.

6.3Conflict of Interest

  • Staff must disclose personal conflicts that may affect a moderation decision.
  • A staff member should not independently investigate a complaint in which they are directly involved.
  • Another authorised staff member should review serious complaints involving staff.

6.4Administrative Accountability

  • Important moderation actions should have a recorded reason.
  • Serious punishments should be supported by available evidence.
  • Staff decisions may be reviewed by authorised leadership.
  • Staff members are subject to the same conduct rules as ordinary members.

6.5Inactivity & Role Review

  • Staff members should communicate extended absences.
  • Repeated failure to fulfil assigned responsibilities may lead to a role review.
  • Promotions and removals should be based on performance, conduct and organisational needs.
VII

ADVERTISING, SCAMS & PLATFORM ABUSE

11 clauses

7.1Unauthorised Advertising

  • Unapproved server invitations, promotions and referral links are prohibited.
  • Mass advertising and unsolicited promotional messages are not permitted.
  • Partnerships and promotional campaigns require prior authorisation.

7.2Scams & Fraud

  • Fake giveaways, phishing links and fraudulent offers are prohibited.
  • Do not impersonate payment services, game developers or official organisations.
  • Do not request money through deceptive claims.
  • Report suspected scams immediately.

7.3Raids & Disruption

  • Coordinated spam, mass mentions and organised channel disruption are prohibited.
  • Do not encourage raids against other Discord servers.
  • Do not organise harassment campaigns against individuals or communities.
  • Do not misuse bots or automated tools to disrupt services.
VIII

EVENTS, REWARDS & HALL OF FAME

12 clauses

8.1Official Events

  • Follow the published event rules and eligibility requirements.
  • Event hosts must communicate schedules and requirements clearly.
  • Participants must respect hosts, referees and other competitors.
  • Event results must not be manipulated or falsely reported.

8.2Rewards & Recognition

  • Rewards are issued according to the published event conditions.
  • Do not falsely claim a title, reward or championship.
  • Staff must not secretly alter results to favour friends.
  • Recognition may be withheld or reviewed if an achievement is found to be fraudulent.

8.3Hall of Fame Integrity

  • Only verified achievements may be officially inducted.
  • Player names, handles, games, events and achievement records should be accurate.
  • Corrections may be requested through official channels.
  • Featured status is an editorial recognition and does not change a player's moderation privileges.
IX

DISCIPLINARY ACTION & APPEALS

17 clauses

9.1Available Actions

Depending on the severity and evidence, ZERO-MERCY may apply:

  • 1. Verbal reminder.
  • 2. Official warning.
  • 3. Temporary restriction.
  • 4. Timeout.
  • 5. Event or roster suspension.
  • 6. Temporary server ban.
  • 7. Permanent server ban.
  • 8. Staff or organisational role removal.

9.2Serious Violations

Severe cases involving child exploitation, credible threats, doxxing, account compromise, serious harassment or organised attacks may result in immediate protective action, including permanent removal.

A warning is not required before action in serious safety cases.

9.3Fair Enforcement

  • Decisions should consider the severity, context, evidence and history of the incident.
  • Similar cases should be treated consistently.
  • Staff must not punish members based on personal dislike.
  • False or deliberately malicious reports may themselves be reviewed.

9.4Appeals

  • Members may submit an appeal through the official support system.
  • Appeals should include the relevant action and a clear explanation.
  • An uninvolved authorised reviewer should handle appeals where possible.
  • Appeals do not guarantee reversal of a decision.
  • Safety restrictions may remain active during review.

OFFICIAL ORGANISATIONAL PRINCIPLES

8 clauses

Every ZERO-MERCY member is expected to uphold these principles:

DISCIPLINE — Maintain control over words and actions.

INTEGRITY — Be honest about identity, achievements and conduct.

RESPECT — Treat members, opponents and staff fairly.

ACCOUNTABILITY — Accept responsibility for decisions and mistakes.

CONFIDENTIALITY — Protect authorised internal information.

FAIR PLAY — Compete through skill, teamwork and legitimate methods.

SAFETY — Protect the community from harassment, exploitation and abuse.

END

FINAL DECLARATION

5 clauses

ZERO-MERCY is built on discipline, competitive integrity, mutual respect and a shared commitment to its community.

Membership is a responsibility, not merely a title. Every member, regardless of rank, is expected to uphold these standards.

No position grants immunity from the rules. No achievement excuses misconduct. No individual is above accountability.

Frequently asked questions

What happens if I break a rule?

Action is chosen from the disciplinary ladder in Section 9.1 — from a verbal reminder up to a permanent ban. Severity, context, evidence and your history all count.

Do I always get a warning first?

No. For serious safety cases — child exploitation, credible threats, doxxing, account compromise, organised attacks — protective action can be taken immediately, without a warning (Section 9.2).

How do I appeal a punishment?

Through the official support system. Include the action taken and a clear explanation. Where possible an uninvolved staff member reviews it. An appeal does not guarantee a reversal, and safety restrictions may stay active during review (Section 9.4).

Can I advertise my own server or channel?

Not without prior authorisation. Unapproved invites, referral links, mass advertising and unsolicited promotion are prohibited. Partnerships need approval before anything is posted (Section 7.1).

Someone is harassing me. What should I do?

Report it through the official moderation or support system instead of replying in public. Do not expose the person or the evidence publicly, and do not retaliate — retaliation against a good-faith report is itself a violation (Sections 4.5, 1.1).

Can staff punish me for personal reasons?

No. Punishing without a legitimate reason, using permissions for revenge, threatening members with administrative action, or deleting evidence to protect a friend are all prohibited. Staff are subject to the same conduct rules as everyone else (Sections 6.2, 6.4).

How do I get into the competitive roster?

Through an application, invitation or assessment. Selection looks at conduct, reliability, skill and suitability. No applicant is guaranteed acceptance, and false achievements or bribery end the process immediately (Sections 3.1–3.3).

What counts as cheating?

Cheats, hacks, scripts, unauthorised modifications, match fixing, fake screenshots, fabricated records, unauthorised account sharing and deliberately disrupting official matches (Section 2.4).

Can I record voice calls or share screenshots?

Not private voice conversations without consent, and never edited screenshots used to falsely accuse someone. Evidence in reports must not be altered or misrepresented (Section 5.4).

Is there an age requirement?

Discord's own minimum age applies to everyone here. Separately, ZERO-MERCY has a zero-tolerance policy on any sexual content involving minors — it is reported to Discord and the authorities (Section 4.1).

Can I share someone's personal information?

Never without permission. Real names, addresses, phone numbers, emails, credentials, private conversations and doxxing are all covered — publishing or threatening to publish private information is strictly prohibited (Sections 5.1, 4.3).

Why is the rulebook so strict?

Because a competitive community runs on predictability. Members should know exactly what is allowed and what is not, and staff should apply the same standard to everyone — including themselves (Sections 6.4, 9.3).
